How it works

Pollution starts between the monitors. Now it gets noticed.

Delhi has about 40 official monitors for 1,500 km². A garbage fire two kilometres from the nearest one never shows up on the official map — until someone who can see it speaks up.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an account to report?

No. Open the app for your state and report. If you like, you can verify your phone number later so your reports stay with you when you change phones.

What happens after I send a report?

Within seconds you see what was found and advice to stay safe. When other reports or sensor data confirm it, the official responsible for that area is alerted, and you can follow the status under My Reports.

Who can see my photo?

Officials of your own state. Your report never leaves your state's system: other states receive only anonymised weekly summaries.

Which areas are covered?

Delhi NCR (Delhi and neighbouring districts of Haryana, Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan) and the Mumbai–Pune corridor. Each state runs its own service, so more states can join.

I'm an official. How do I get access?

Your state administrator creates your account. Sign in on your state's officials' dashboard with the email and password they give you.
