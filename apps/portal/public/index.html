From a photo to an alert

Delhi has ~40 official monitors for 1,500 km². Pollution starts between them.

A garbage fire two kilometres from the nearest monitor never shows up on the official map. VayuSetu makes every citizen a sensor — and makes sure someone acts.

Gemini reads the air

A second citizen tips the scale

Independent reports from the same spot are fused with an AutoML model over Sentinel-5P, MODIS fire and weather. Two distinct people push the cell past the alert line — one phone can never do it alone.
